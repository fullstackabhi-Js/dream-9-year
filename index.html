9th class, home tuition

No bench, no board — just a small home tuition and two students who kept getting seated together. We tried changing seats more than once. Fate quietly put us back side by side, every single time.

the concepts I never got the first time

You were the one who explained things until they finally made sense to me — patiently, without ever making me feel slow for asking twice. I think some part of me liked not understanding, just to hear you explain it again.

outside your school gate

You'd call me to come meet you there, and I'd walk the whole way just for a few minutes with you. And on the days we took an auto back, you'd pay the fare before I could even reach for my pocket. Small thing. I never forgot it.

11th class, a new stream

I chose commerce — not because I loved it, but because it meant I could switch tuitions and be in the same one as you. Still the best subject decision I never regret.

ten minutes, every day

We started going early, just to get a few private minutes before anyone else arrived. Ten minutes a day, and somehow it was always the best part of mine.

27th December, 2017 — the stairs

You were the one who said it first — "let's hug." And it happened, unplanned, right there on the stairs, on our way back home. I still remember exactly how that moment felt. Hugs came easier after that. Then, one day, a kiss on the cheek — still one of the best moments of my life. And you still love teasing me until I giggle, pressing my waist just to watch me lose it.

the fights, the short breakups

We fought. We broke up, briefly, more than once. And every single time, we came back steadier — more patient, more understanding, more in love than before, not less.

today

Studies done, jobs done, and we're still here — still talking, still sharing everything. You still get your mood swings, and I try, as patiently as I can, to hold you through them — because underneath it, you're just so cute and so innocent, and entirely worth the patience.
